Numvine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Developer: TheLazyMind

Numvine is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Numvine does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Numvine does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (completed levels, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, remaining hints) and your settings (sound and vibration) on your own device so that you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access and third parties

Numvine works fully offline. The current version does not connect to the internet, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not show advertisements. It does not share any data with third parties.

Changes in future versions

If a future version adds features that use data, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the changes will be described here.

Children

Because Numvine collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email numbervine0@gmail.com.
