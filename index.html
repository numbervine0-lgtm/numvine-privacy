Numvine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Developer: TheLazyMind

Numvine is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Playing without an account

You can play Numvine fully offline and without an account. In that case the game saves your progress (completed levels, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, remaining hints) and your settings (sound, vibration, theme) on your own device only. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

Signing in is optional and is used only to back up and restore your progress. Sign-in is provided by Google through Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google. We store your game progress (levels, stars, times, daily results and hints) in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), linked to your user ID, so it can be restored on this or another phone.

We use this information only to provide the backup and restore feature. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than Google, which processes it on our behalf as our cloud provider.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and the progress stored in the cloud. If you cannot open the app, email numbervine0@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account with the subject “Delete my Numvine account”, and we will delete your account and its data. Progress saved on your own phone stays there until you reset it in Settings or uninstall the app.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of Numvine does not show advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking tools. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the changes will be described here.

Children

Numvine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app, with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email numbervine0@gmail.com.
